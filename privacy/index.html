ChromaBay Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 15, 2026

ChromaBay is an app by ReVolt Labs for designing lighting patterns and controlling addressable-LED devices. This policy explains what data the app handles and why. We keep it minimal: we don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

Information we collect

  • Account information. If you create an account, we collect your email address and a password (stored securely by our authentication provider). This is used only to create and sign you into your account.
  • Your patterns. Patterns you create are stored on your device. If you sign in, they sync to your private library on our servers so they're available across your devices. Patterns you explicitly choose to publish to the community gallery become public and are shown alongside a name/handle you provide.
  • Diagnostics. We use a crash-reporting service to collect crash logs and basic technical details (such as app version, device model, and OS version) so we can find and fix problems. This is not used to identify you.
  • Bluetooth. The app uses Bluetooth only to communicate locally with your own LED controllers. We do not collect or transmit data from these connections.

How we use information

To provide and operate the app: sign-in, syncing your pattern library, powering the optional community gallery, and diagnosing crashes. That's it. We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Third-party services

We rely on a small number of service providers that process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, and storage for your account, synced library, and the gallery.
  • Sentry — crash and error reporting.

Data retention and your choices

  • You can delete individual patterns at any time, and sign out to stop syncing.
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated data by emailing us (below); we'll remove it within a reasonable period.

Children

ChromaBay is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll revise the date at the top of this page.

Questions about this policy or your data? Contact ReVolt Labs at grant@revoltlabs.co.
